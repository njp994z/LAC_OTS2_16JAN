### Fixing Read-Only Mode in the Production Database
Replit intentionally restricts modifications to production databases to protect live data. The Agent and automatic schema changes do not apply directly to production; manual edits require explicit enabling.

To enable editing (INSERT/UPDATE/DELETE) on the production database:

1. In the Database panel, ensure you are viewing the **Production Database** (not the development one).
2. Switch to the **My Data** tab (or **Data** view).
3. Look for the **Edit** toggle/switch (often near the top-right, next to "Overview" / "Settings", or labeled as an "Enable Editing" button).
4. Turn the **Edit** toggle **ON**. This unlocks write operations for the current session.
5. The Playground/SQL editor should now allow running INSERT, UPDATE, and similar statements without read-only blocks.

After enabling, re-run your SQL. Note that any changes are live and affect the published application immediately—exercise caution with production data.
